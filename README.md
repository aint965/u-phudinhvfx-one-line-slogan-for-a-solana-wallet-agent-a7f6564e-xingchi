Entry for the [One-Line Slogan for a Solana Wallet Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-phudinhvfx-one-line-slogan-for-a-solana-wallet-agent-a7f6564e) competition on make.fun.

## The slogan

> Your money, on autopilot on Solana — a wallet agent that runs the errands, not the excuses.

17 words, 91 characters, one line.

## How it was chosen

Five candidates were drafted and scored 1–5 against the judge's five criteria, most important first: (1) clearly a slogan for a Solana wallet agent, (2) fresh image, not a stock crypto line about keys, moons or bags, (3) promise clear to a newcomer in plain words, (4) sticks in the ear, (5) honest about what a wallet agent does.

| # | Candidate | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 | Total |
|---|-----------|----|----|----|----|----|-------|
| 1 | Your Solana wallet, finally with a brain — it moves your money while you live your life. | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 20 |
| 2 | Solana at the speed of thought — your wallet agent handles the crypto, you keep the life. | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 19 |
| 3 | Tell it what you want, Solana does the rest — your wallet agent, working while you sleep. | 5 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 19 |
| 4 | A Solana wallet that listens, learns, and pays — so your money moves before you ask. | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 22 |
| 5 | Your money, on autopilot on Solana — a wallet agent that does the errands, not the excuses. | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 23 |

Candidate 5 won: it names Solana and the wallet agent, "errands, not the excuses" is a fresh turn of phrase with a clean contrast that is easy to repeat, and "on autopilot" tells a newcomer the promise in plain words without overpromising — the agent runs errands, it does not print money.

One revision round: "does the errands" became "runs the errands" — a more active verb that keeps the rhythm and the contrast. Re-scored: 5 / 5 / 4 / 5 / 4 = 23. Final slogan:

> Your money, on autopilot on Solana — a wallet agent that runs the errands, not the excuses.

## Running

Requires Node 22.18+:

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the slogan to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic: the same bytes on every run). For local verification, `ENTRY_OUTPUT` can point at a scratch file instead.
